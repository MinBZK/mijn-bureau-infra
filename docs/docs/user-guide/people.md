# People

People is the MijnBureau staff directory: one place that answers "who is this,
what do they do, and how do I reach them". It is fed from the same central
accounts as the rest of the platform, so names, e-mail addresses and avatars stay
consistent.

## Opening it

- Web: `https://people.<global.domain>`
- From the [Bureaublad](./bureaublad.md): the people tile

## What you find there

| Section      | Contents                                                   |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Directory    | All colleagues, searchable by name, team or role           |
| Teams        | Groups of people, usually mirroring the organisation chart |
| Profile card | Name, photo, e-mail, phone, job title, team                |
| Contacts     | People you have saved for quick access                     |
| Your profile | The details you maintain about yourself                    |

## Finding a colleague

1. Type a name, part of a name or a team in the search field.
2. Filter by team or job title if you are unsure of the spelling.
3. Open the profile card.
4. From the card you can start a [chat](./element.md), send
   [mail](./messages.md) or copy the address into another tool.

Search is deliberately simple: it does not show private information such as home
addresses or personnel files.

## Updating your own details

1. Open your profile card (usually via your name or avatar).
2. Choose _Edit_.
3. Update the fields your organisation allows you to change — typically job
   title, phone number, location and photo.
4. Save. Your name and e-mail address come from the central account and are
   therefore not editable here; change those in [your account](./account.md).

Keeping your photo and job title current is not cosmetic: colleagues use the
directory to decide who to ask.

## Teams

- Teams group people and are used for sharing in [Docs](./docs.md),
  [Drive](./drive.md) and [Projects](./projects.md).
- Being in the right team therefore grants access to the right shared material —
  tell your administrator if you are missing from a team.
- Team descriptions explain what the team does and who to contact for what.

## Sharing and privacy

- Only colleagues with an account can see the directory. There is no public
  profile page.
- Your contact details are visible to everybody in the organisation; that is the
  point of a directory, so keep personal numbers out of it.
- The directory does not show mail content, calendar entries or files — only
  what is in your profile.

## Good to know

- Accounts are created by your administrator; People mirrors them, it does not
  create them.
- When someone leaves, their profile is deactivated together with the account.
  Reassign their files and mailboxes before that happens if the work continues.
- The directory is the authoritative list of colleagues, so use it instead of
  asking in chat who is responsible for something.
