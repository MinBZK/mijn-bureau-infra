** Please be patient while the chart is being deployed **

The Keycloak realm configuration is imported by a Job that runs as a Helm
hook after every install, upgrade or rollback.

Watch the import Job with:

  kubectl get jobs --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . | quote }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

Get the logs of the import with:

  kubectl logs --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . | quote }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }},app.kubernetes.io/component=keycloak-config-cli
